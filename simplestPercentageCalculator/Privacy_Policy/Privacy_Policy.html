Short summary

  • We operate no servers, no accounts and no backend. We never receive, store, or see the numbers you type.
  • Your "Recent" calculation history is saved only in local storage on your own device and can be cleared by you at any time.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

a. Calculation history ("Recent")

To let you revisit recent work, the App saves your most recent calculations (the selected mode, the numbers you entered, and the result) in your device's own local storage — AsyncStorage on Android and iOS, and the browser's localStorage on the web — under the key @spc/calculation-history. A maximum of 20 entries is kept; older entries are automatically discarded.

This data never leaves your device, is not accessible to us, and is not backed up to any service we control. (Your own operating system or browser may include it in backups or syncing that you have enabled, such as Android Auto Backup or iCloud device backups; that data is handled under Google's or Apple's own policies.)

Deleting it: tap Clear in the Recent card inside the App, uninstall the App, clear the App's storage in your device settings, or clear your browser's site data for the web version.

b. Clipboard

When you tap Copy result, the App writes the displayed result to your device clipboard. This happens only in response to your action. The App does not read your clipboard and does not monitor its contents.

c. Ad-related local data

Google's advertising software may store limited technical data on your device (for example frequency-capping or consent state) as part of serving ads. This is controlled by Google, not by us, and is described in the policies linked in Section 4.

3. Advertising on Android and iOS (Google AdMob)

The Android and iOS versions of the App are supported by advertising delivered through Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC, depending on your location). Two ad formats are used: a banner at the bottom of the screen and an occasional full-screen (interstitial) ad shown after a number of calculations.

When an ad is requested or displayed, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process, automatically:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Google Advertising ID, or Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) when you have permitted tracking on iOS);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate (non-precise) location such as country or region may be derived;
  • device and software information, such as device model, manufacturer, operating system and version, language, time zone, screen size, and network type;
  • ad interaction and performance data, such as whether an ad was served, viewed, or clicked, and diagnostic/anti-fraud signals;
  • the app identifier and app version in which the ad was shown.

This information is collected directly by Google through its SDK and is processed under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We receive only aggregated, non-identifying performance and revenue reports (for example impressions, clicks, and earnings) in the AdMob dashboard. We cannot identify individual users from those reports.

The web version of the App contains no advertising code. AdMob and Apple tracking components are not loaded on the web at all.

6. Your Choices & How to Control Ad Tracking

  • Android: open Settings → Google → Ads (path may vary by device) to delete or reset your advertising ID and to opt out of ad personalization. Ads may still be shown, but they will be non-personalized.
  • iOS: the first time ads are prepared, iOS asks for your permission to track you across apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). You may decline; you can change your decision at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking or Settings → Percentage Calculator. If you decline, the IDFA is not used and ads are non-personalized.
  • EEA, UK, and Switzerland: where consent is legally required for personalized advertising, ads are intended to be served on a non-personalized basis unless valid consent has been obtained through the consent mechanism presented by Google on our behalf. You may withdraw or change your choice through your device ad settings as described above.
  • Local data: clear your Recent history inside the App, or uninstall the App, to remove all locally stored data.
  • Ad-free use: the web version of the App provides the full calculator with no advertising.
